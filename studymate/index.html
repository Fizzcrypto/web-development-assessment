PLAN SMALL. FINISH STRONG.

Make room for what matters.

StudyMate is a simple student planning website made for people who want to keep their college work in one place. When several subjects have assignments due in the same week, it can be easy to forget a date or feel unsure about what to do first. StudyMate brings tasks, deadlines and study plans together so you can see what needs attention. You do not need an account or special software to get started.

Open My Tasks to add an assignment, choose its due date and mark it complete when you finish. Use the Study Planner to arrange study sessions across the week and build a realistic routine. Your entries are saved in this browser, making the site useful for everyday planning. StudyMate is designed to be clear, easy to navigate and comfortable to use on a phone or computer.
